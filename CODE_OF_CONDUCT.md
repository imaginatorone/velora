# Code of Conduct

Velora should be a place where people can do careful technical work without
being dismissed, harassed, or pressured.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking the person behind them.
- Give specific feedback and accept correction without hostility.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and communication styles.
- Keep public discussion relevant to the project.
- Step back when a conversation stops being productive.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized attention.
- Personal insults, deliberate intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated contact after someone has asked for it to stop.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not open a public issue about a private conduct matter. Contact a lead
maintainer through the private contact method listed on the
[`@imaginatorone`](https://github.com/imaginatorone) or
[`@Lucferdev`](https://github.com/Lucferdev) GitHub profile.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports
will be reviewed in context, kept private as far as practical, and handled
without retaliation against a good-faith reporter.

This code applies to project spaces and to public conduct that directly
affects participation in the project.
